The problem, and how it was decomposed

The failure mode is not slow. It is confidently wrong.

Preparing for a new city means days of reading government portals, statistics, papers and policy documents — ending in a document in one person’s folder that nobody can verify or reuse. The risk that matters is walking into a room of health officials with a national statistic quoted as a city one, a programme that ended years ago, or a figure nobody can source. So the goal is not a summariser; it is a research system whose answers can be interrogated.

“Understanding a city” is committed to five dimensions, enforced by the workflow: planning queries each one and the sufficiency gate measures coverage against them.

01 · BurdenDisease and riskCVD, hypertension, diabetes, dyslipidaemia.
02 · ProgrammesWhat already runsAnd who runs it.
03 · PolicyGovernanceMunicipal and national initiatives in force.
04 · AccessDeliveryFacilities, workforce, pathways to care.
05 · ActorsInstitutionsOrganisations in the system.

Deliberate exclusion: the system never characterises named individuals or their attitudes — exactly the claims public sources cannot support.

02 / 08
AI solution and architecture

Ten agents. None both produces a claim and approves it.

Intake→Plan→Discover→ Crawl gate→Extract→Claims→ Fact check→Sufficiency→Index→Report
↺ Sufficiency → Plan when fewer than 3 of 5 dimensions have city-level evidence · bounded by the chosen depth
Gate · before crawlingCrawlabilityrobots.txt is read before the first byte is fetched; a refused source stays listed with its reason.
ProducerClaim extractionThe only agent allowed to phrase a claim — never allowed to publish one. Reads HTML, full-text XML and PDF.
Gate · before publishingIndependent fact checkNever the model that extracted the claim. Can return UNSUPPORTED or SCOPE_MISMATCH, which withhold or flag.
Gate · before the briefSufficiencySpends more research budget, or publishes with every gap declared.

Discovery uses keyless public APIs — OpenAlex, Europe PMC, Wikipedia — rather than scraped search engines. GET /api/workflow renders this structure from the compiled graph, so the diagram cannot drift from the code.

04 / 08
Trustworthiness and evidence

No located quote, no fact.

Every finding carries its source URL and title, a verbatim quote, geographic scope, verdict, the model that verified it, and a confidence. The strongest guarantees are mechanical — they do not depend on a model behaving well.

Guard 01 · groundingThe quote must exist in the sourceThe model’s quote is searched for in the full document and the stored span is the one found there. An unlocatable quote is discarded.
Guard 02 · numericFigures must appear in the evidenceA number in the claim that is absent from its quote is automatically UNSUPPORTED — no model opinion involved.
Guard 03 · scopeNational ≠ cityScope is a field on every fact. National evidence is published only with an explicit “not a city figure” warning.
Unknowns are outputA gap ledger beside the findingsWithheld, blocked, unreachable and missing evidence each land there with a reason — the questions to take into the meeting.
Degraded runs say soNo silent downgradeA fact checked by rules instead of a model raises a guardrail warning and is counted; a runtime banner names any reduced capability.

Engineering decisions · measured before made

Trust is never traded. Time, tokens and cost are.

DecisionWhyEvidence
Open-weights on GroqFree, fast, no lock-in — the same models run on Ollama or vLLM. A paid API added cost per run; a local 14B model does not fit 8 GB.Graphiti reached OpenAI in 3 places regardless of config; all replaced
A model per jobExtraction rotates across qwen3.8-27b, gpt-oss-120b and gpt-oss-20b; checking uses any model except the extractor; the graph has its own.~3,500 tokens per extraction call vs 8,000 per minute per model
Deterministic firstrobots.txt, quote grounding and the numeric guard run before any model; a failing claim never costs checker tokens.Relevance windowing sends 6,000 chars, not the first 14,000
Strict schemas, low reasoningSchema-constrained verdicts and low effort on the checker model.3× fewer tokens, 3.4× faster, 16/16 valid (was 5/16 empty)
A rate limit is not an outageShared per-model cooldown, failover to the model free soonest, errors classified by code, not by message words.Once 75 rejected requests to land 16; extraction 383 s → seconds
Brief first, graph laterResearch is a background job the UI polls; Graphiti builds after the brief is returned.A dropped connection no longer loses the result

07 / 08
Trade-offs and limitations

Working end to end beats half-built ambition.

What we cutHuman review, OCR, authenticationThe fact-check gate plus a visible gap ledger buys most of a review queue’s safety for a fraction of the time. Scanned PDFs are flagged as needing OCR.
What free tiers costLatency and a sleeping appRate limits set the pace of extraction; the first request after idle is slow; SQLite history resets on restart while vectors and graph persist.
Not modelledConflicting sourcesTwo sources that disagree both appear with their quotes. Adjudicating contradiction is real work, consciously deferred.
NextDurability, then depthPostgreSQL and authentication; a paid tier to lift rate limits; contradiction detection; a true cross-encoder reranker.
