CARDIO4Cities / AI engineer case study

Understand a city before the first meeting.

An evidence-first research workspace that prepares a City Lead for government and healthcare conversations — built so that every sentence can be traced back to a source, and everything unknown stays visible.

Live research · LangGraph · three datastores 01 / 08
The problem as we understand it

Public knowledge is everywhere. Confidence is not.

Preparing for a new city today means days of manual reading across government portals, health statistics, academic papers, policy documents and stakeholder networks. The output is a document in one person’s folder: fragmented, hard to verify, impossible to reuse when the next city joins.

The failure mode that matters is not slow. It is confidently wrong in a room full of health officials — a national statistic quoted as a city one, a programme that ended two years ago, a name nobody can source.

So the goal is not a summariser. It is a research system whose answers can be interrogated.
02 / 08
Problem decomposition

What “understanding a city” was decomposed into.

An open question needs a committed answer. We defined city understanding as five retrievable dimensions, and every research plan expands into queries across them.

01BurdenCardiovascular risk, hypertension, diabetes, dyslipidaemia.
02ProgrammesWhat already runs, and who runs it.
03PolicyMunicipal and national initiatives in force.
04AccessFacilities, workforce, pathways to care.
05ActorsOrganisations and institutions in the system.

Deliberate exclusion: the system never characterises named individuals or their attitudes. Those are exactly the claims a public source cannot support, and the ones most damaging to get wrong.

AI and agentic design

No agent both produces a claim and approves it.

Gate — before crawlingCrawlability agentrobots.txt is read before the first byte is fetched. A disallowed source is still listed, with its refusal reason, so the user can follow it by hand.
Gate — before publishingIndependent fact checkerSeparate from the agent that produced the claim, and able to conclude “unsupported”. Withheld claims never reach the brief.
ConsequenceRefusals become outputEvery block lands in the knowledge gap ledger with topic, reason and URL — shown next to the findings, not hidden in a log.

Trustworthiness and evidence

No quote, no fact.

Every finding carries a source URL, a source title, a verbatim evidence quote, a geographic scope, a verification status and a confidence. “Where did this come from?” is one click, on every row.

FabricationNothing is inventedNo statistic, person or attitude is generated. If the evidence is not in a retrieved source, the claim does not exist.
ScopeNational ≠ cityScope is a field on every record. Unresolved scope is flagged in the open, not silently assumed to be city-level.
AbsenceUnknowns are output“Insufficient verified evidence was found” is a valid, useful answer — and the gap ledger says what is still missing.
The thing a City Lead can take into the room is not the summary. It is the citation underneath it.
06 / 08
User experience

Type a city. Follow the evidence.

The user is a City Lead, not an engineer. Progress is narrated in plain language while the run is live, and the workspace opens on findings — not on infrastructure.

  • 01Start. One field. Any city, including one never researched before.
  • 02Watch. Plain-language progress: finding sources, checking access, reading, checking evidence.
  • 03Read. Findings and knowledge gaps side by side, each expandable to its quote and source.
  • 04Explore. The knowledge graph shows how organisations, programmes and policies connect.
  • 05Ask. Questions answered from verified evidence only, with the citations attached.
  • 06Take it with you. A downloadable brief, and a history that makes the next run cumulative.
Runtime and evaluation metrics live behind a separate admin tab 07 / 08
Trade-offs and limitations

Working end to end beats half-built ambition.

What we cutBreadth, and a human review queueFew topic queries, few sources per run, no reviewer step. The fact-check gate plus a visible gap ledger buys most of the safety for a fraction of the time.
What is still shallowClaim extraction and verificationFindings are closer to source-level than statement-level, and the verifier reasons about evidence shape more than evidence meaning. This is the first thing to deepen.
Not modelledConflicting sourcesTwo sources that disagree both appear with their quotes. Detecting and adjudicating contradiction is real work, consciously deferred.
NextDepth, then durabilityGrounded claim extraction, graph traversal inside the answer path, a second discovery source, managed PostgreSQL, authentication.

Demo path: pick an unseen city → walk the workflow → open a finding to its quote → inspect the gaps → explore the graph → ask a question → download the brief.
