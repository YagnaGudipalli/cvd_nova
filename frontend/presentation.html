The problem, and how it was decomposed

Today
  • Days of reading portals, statistics, papers, policy
  • Ends as one person’s document — unverifiable, not reusable
Real risk: confidently wrong
  • A national statistic quoted as a city one
  • A programme that ended years ago
  • A figure nobody can source
So the goal
  • Not a summariser — research whose answers can be interrogated
  • Out of scope: views of named individuals
“Understanding a city” = 5 dimensions, enforced by the workflow
#DimensionExample question
01BurdenHow common are CVD, hypertension, diabetes?
02ProgrammesWhat screening already runs, and who runs it?
03PolicyWhich municipal or national NCD plans are in force?
04AccessFacilities, workforce, pathways to care?
05ActorsWhich institutions matter?

Planner queries each dimension; the sufficiency gate measures coverage against them.

AI solution and architecture

Intake→Plan→Discover→ Crawl gate→Extract→Claims→ Fact check→Sufficiency→Index→Report
↺ Sufficiency → Plan if < 3 of 5 dimensions have city evidence · bounded by depth
AgentDoesNairobi example
PlanQueries per dimension“Nairobi Kenya hypertension screening programme”
DiscoverKeyless APIs: OpenAlex, Europe PMC, Wikipedia75 candidate sources over 2 rounds
gate Crawlrobots.txt before first byte; refusals listed58 approved, 17 kept aside
Extract → ClaimsReads HTML, XML, PDF; only agent that phrases a claim26 candidate claims
gate Fact checkNever the extracting model; SUPPORTED / UNSUPPORTED / SCOPE_MISMATCH25 published, 1 withheld
gate SufficiencyResearch more, or publish with gaps declaredRound 2 triggered, then published
Index → ReportVectors, graph, brief25 facts indexed; brief + 21 gaps

Rule: no agent both produces a claim and approves it. GET /api/workflow draws this from the compiled graph.

Knowledge management and retrieval

StoreHoldsAnswersNairobi run
SQLiteRuns, sources, crawl decisions, facts, gaps, traces, metrics“What did the system decide, and when?”100 records
QdrantEvidence passages + provenance, city-filtered“What evidence is near my question?”25 facts
Graphiti + Neo4jOrganisations, Programmes, Policies, Indicators, Places“Who is connected to what, since when?”Built after the brief
Question→Qdrant + Graphiti, concurrently→Rerank→Numbered evidence→Cited answer or decline
  • Citations resolve to stored records — never model memory
  • Collection named after the embedder: vectors from two models never mix
  • Idempotent point IDs: re-running a city does not duplicate evidence
  • Ledger is the fallback when vector or graph is unavailable

Engineering decisions

Trust is never traded. Time, tokens and cost are.

DecisionWhatMeasured effect
Open-weights on GroqFree, no lock-in; replaced 3 hidden OpenAI calls in Graphiti$0 per run
A model per jobExtraction rotates 3 models; checker ≠ extractor~3.5k tokens/call within 8k/min limits
Deterministic firstrobots, grounding, numeric guard before any model6k relevant chars sent, not first 14k
Strict schemasSchema-constrained verdicts, low reasoning effort3× fewer tokens · 3.4× faster · 16/16 valid (was 5/16)
